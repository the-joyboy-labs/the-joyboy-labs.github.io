Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 25, 2026

The short version: we don’t run accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we don’t collect personal information. Here’s the detail.

01The short version

The Joyboy Labs is a static website. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it.

02What we collect

We do not directly collect personal information. We do not operate our own server, database or access logs.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details when you load a page, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not receive that data in a form that identifies individual visitors.

03Optional: live GitHub star count

The homepage can show a live star count. To do that, your browser contacts api.github.com, which means GitHub can see your IP address and browser details.

We only make this request after you choose “Accept” in the privacy banner. If you choose “Decline”, the request is never made. You can change your mind at any time from Cookie settings in the footer.

04What is stored in your browser

The only thing we store is your privacy choice (accept or decline) and the date you made it, in your browser’s local storage, so we don’t ask you every visit. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent to us. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

05Interactive demos

Demos and simulators on this site run entirely in your browser. Text you type into them is not transmitted anywhere by us. The pages we host do not request access to your camera, microphone or location.

06Links to other sites

We link to services such as Discord, GitHub, Roblox, OWASP and others. Once you leave this site, their privacy practices apply, not ours.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or India), you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you are welcome to ask. You can withdraw your consent at any time through Cookie settings.

08Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

09Changes and contact

If our practices change (for example if we add analytics), we will update this page and the date above, and ask for consent where required.

Questions or requests? Reach us on Discord or open an issue at our GitHub organization.
