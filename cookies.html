Cookie Policy

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 25, 2026

We don’t set cookies. This page explains exactly what is stored in your browser, and the one optional request you control.

01What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. They are often used for logins, preferences and tracking.

02What this site uses

We set no cookies and use no advertising, analytics or tracking technologies. The only item we store is your privacy choice:

NameTypePurposeDuration
joyboy-consentLocal storage (first party)Remembers whether you accepted or declined the optional GitHub star-count request, and when.Up to 12 months, then we ask again

This item is needed to respect your choice, so it is stored regardless of whether you accept or decline.

03The one optional request

If you click Accept, the homepage fetches the live star count from api.github.com. This is not a cookie, but it does let GitHub see your IP address, so we treat it as consent-based. Decline and it never happens.

04Third parties

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you follow links to Discord, GitHub or other sites, those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies.

05Managing your choice

Use the button below to reopen the privacy banner and change your answer at any time. You can also clear this site’s local storage in your browser settings.

06Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us on Discord or open an issue at our GitHub organization.
