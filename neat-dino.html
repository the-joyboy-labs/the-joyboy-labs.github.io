// THE ENGINEERING FIX

Why v2 Dinos Jumped Birds—and How v3 Solved It

Standard NEAT T-Rex implementations allow agents to cheese flying birds by jumping over them or staying perpetually crouched. Version 3 re-engineered the physics and reward structure so true bird-ducking emerges naturally.

01 / HEAD-HEIGHT BIRDS

Pterodactyls at yPos 70 & 75

Flying birds spawn strictly at head-and-torso height (y = 70..103). A standing T-Rex (y = 93..137) collides head-on unless it crouches.

02 / AIRSPACE BARRIER

No jumping over birds

Each Pterodactyl carries an upper airspace collision box (CollisionBox(4, -80, 38, 92)) blocking y = 0..103. Jumping into a bird is an instant crash.

03 / CROUCH CLEARANCE

Ducking & 3x fast-drop

Ducking lowers the T-Rex hitbox to y = 112..137, sliding cleanly below the bird's y = 103 belly. Mid-air ducking triggers a 3x gravity speed-drop.

04 / ACTIVE POSTURE

Anti-perpetual-crouch

Jumping while ducking is locked out, and ground-ducking when a cactus is ahead is penalized—teaching the AI to stand upright after every bird passes.
